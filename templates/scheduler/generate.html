How it works
  1. Loads active employees, assignments, operating rules, holidays, approved leave and comp-offs.
  2. Rotating-off employees get one weekly off per week on the day that keeps coverage intact, honours their preference, and spreads offs fairly.
  3. Every available employee is rostered on each of their brand-process workloads that operate that day.
  4. Remaining gaps are filled by designated backup resources who are free.
  5. The validator checks every mandatory constraint; unresolved gaps are reported as conflicts, never hidden.
